Honest about results

TallerBoost helps you build the habits associated with healthy growth and good posture: better sleep, regular stretching, balanced nutrition, and stronger postural muscles. Outcomes depend on your age, genetics, and consistency — we don't promise specific height changes.

TallerBoost is a wellness and lifestyle app, not a medical service. Consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting a new exercise, sleep, or nutrition routine.
