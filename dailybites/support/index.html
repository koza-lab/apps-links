Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Today feed work?

During onboarding you pick your topics and a daily reading goal of 5, 10, or 15 minutes. The Today tab uses those to pick ideas from our library of 500 books, and your streak grows each day you reach your goal. You can change topics and your goal anytime in Profile.

What's free and what needs Premium?

One book in every category is free to read in full, free readers get a daily reading allowance in Today, and you can keep up to two shelves of saved ideas. Premium unlocks the whole library with no daily limit and unlimited shelves.

What's the difference between Weekly and Annual?

Both unlock the same Premium access. Weekly renews every week and Annual renews every year; the annual plan costs much less per week. Both are auto-renewing subscriptions, and the current price in your currency is shown on the subscription screen before you buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone → [your name] → Subscriptions → Microlearning – Daily Bites → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Premium access until the end of your billing period.

I subscribed but the app still shows the paywall.

Tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. If that doesn't fix it, email us with the Apple ID email you used for the purchase.

Who writes the ideas?

Each book is broken into short ideas written in our own words, with real examples and a quick quiz. They're summaries for learning and inspiration, not official summaries from the author or publisher, and not a substitute for reading the book.

Where are the book covers from?

Our cover art is original illustration made for each book, not a reproduction of the published cover.

Why don't I hear any sounds?

Sounds play for quiz answers and milestones only. They follow your iPhone's silent switch, and you can turn them off in Profile → Sounds.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email us with what happened and, if you can, the book or idea you were reading at the time. A screenshot helps.

Privacy

Daily Bites collects only the data needed to personalize your reading and keep your progress. We do not sell your data.

  • You don't need an email or phone number; the app uses an anonymous identifier
  • Your topics, goal, saved ideas, and shelves are stored so they stay with you
  • We use Firebase Analytics and crash reporting to improve the app
  • We use the Meta SDK to measure our ad campaigns. Your advertising identifier is shared only if you allow tracking when iOS asks; you can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not see your payment details

We do not run third-party ads inside the app. Read the full Privacy Policy.
