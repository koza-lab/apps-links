Privacy Policy

Microlearning – Daily Bites · Last updated September 25, 2026

Daily Bites respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. If anything is unclear, email us at marketing@koza-lab.co and we'll answer within 24 hours.

What We Collect

You don't need to create an account with your email or phone number. The app signs you in with an anonymous identifier, and collects:

  • Onboarding answers (an optional display name, the topics you pick, your daily reading goal and preferred reading time) to personalize your feed
  • Your saved ideas and shelves, so they stay with you across app launches
  • Reading activity (ideas and books you open, quiz answers, reading time) to track your progress and streaks
  • Usage and crash data to fix bugs and improve the app
  • Subscription status and purchase events
  • Only if you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to measure our ad campaigns (see Advertising Measurement)

We don't collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or Health data. If you save an idea card as an image, the app only adds it to your photo library; it never reads your photos.

How We Use It

  • To show you ideas from the topics you chose and keep your daily goal and streak
  • To keep your saved ideas, shelves, and progress between sessions
  • To manage your subscription
  • To send the reading reminders you turn on (these are scheduled on your device)
  • To improve the app and fix problems
  • To measure which of our ads lead to installs and subscriptions

What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell your data
  • We don't run third-party ads inside the app
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites unless you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • We don't send your reading history, saved ideas, or onboarding answers to advertising partners

Who We Share With

We share data only with services that help us run the app:

  • Google Firebase for anonymous sign-in, cloud storage of your preferences and saved ideas, analytics, and crash reporting
  • RevenueCat to manage subscriptions
  • Meta (Facebook) to measure the performance of our ad campaigns
  • Apple to distribute the app and process payments

We may also share data when required by law or to protect users from harm.

Advertising Measurement

We run ads on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) and use the Meta SDK to understand which ads lead to installs and subscriptions. The app sends Meta a small set of events: completing onboarding, choosing a subscription plan, and starting a subscription or free trial (plan and price). We don't send your reading history, saved ideas, topics, or display name to Meta.

On first launch, iOS asks whether Daily Bites may track you. If you tap Allow, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Meta along with these events, so ads can be linked to installs. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, we don't share the advertising identifier, and Meta receives only anonymized, aggregated data.

You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Meta's own use of this data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy. See also the Google Privacy Policy and the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Change your topics, daily goal, and reminders anytime in Profile
  • Email us at marketing@koza-lab.co with the subject "Delete My Data" to delete your data; we remove it within 30 days
  • Email us to request a copy of your data or to correct it
  • Stop ad tracking anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions

If you're in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA, including the right to object to processing, request portability, and lodge complaints with your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

Data Retention

We keep your preferences, saved ideas, and progress until you ask us to delete them. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies, and we may keep transaction records longer where the law requires it.

Children

Daily Bites is for users aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you're a parent and believe your child has used the app, contact us and we'll delete their data.

Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, encrypted storage at rest) and access controls to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to keep yours safe.

International Transfers

Our service providers are based in various countries including the US and EU. When data is transferred internationally, we rely on standard legal safeguards (such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app before they take effect.
