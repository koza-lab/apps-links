KOZA·LAB Gloura

Privacy Policy

Gloura · Last updated November 19, 2026

Gloura is a skincare app by Koza Lab: a selfie-based skin analysis, a personal routine and Lumi, an AI skincare assistant. This page explains what we collect, why, who processes it and what you can do about it. Questions? Email marketing@koza-lab.co.

What We Collect

  • Account: an anonymous account created on first launch, and your Sign in with Apple identifier and, if you share it, your name and email once you sign in
  • Quiz answers: skin type, concerns, sensitivity, habits (such as sleep, stress and makeup) and restrictions, used to build your analysis and routine
  • Selfie: the photo you take for the skin analysis. See Selfie and Photos below
  • Lumi chats: what you write to Lumi and Lumi's replies, and photos you choose to share in the chat
  • Routine and progress: your routine, completed steps, skin snapshots, favourite and saved products, product ratings and product views
  • Community: prompts, comments, likes and reports you post. Posts and comments are visible to other Gloura users
  • Purchases: subscription status, managed by Apple and RevenueCat
  • Usage and diagnostics: screens and features used, session length, crash logs
  • Ad measurement: onboarding completed, paywall viewed or tapped and subscription started, sent to Meta and TikTok to measure our ads. Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is read only if you allow tracking
  • Approximate location (optional, while using the app): used only to show the UV index. See Location below

Selfie and Photos

How we use your selfie

When you run the skin analysis, your selfie and your quiz answers are sent to Gloura's server and processed by our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude). It returns your undertone estimate, two skincare tips and a short summary, which Gloura uses to build your Glow Score and routine. Gloura does not use facial recognition, does not identify you from your face, and does not use your selfie for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored

Your latest selfie is stored in Gloura's cloud storage (Google Firebase Storage) under your account, so your analysis can be shown again. Anthropic processes the selfie to answer the request and may not use it to train its models; Gloura's server does not keep a copy of what it sends.

Photos in Lumi

Photos you share with Lumi (for example a product label) are sent to Anthropic to answer you and are not stored by Gloura. Later messages only note that a photo was shared earlier.

How long we keep it

Your selfie is kept until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes it from our storage.

Lumi and AI Features

Lumi, the routine planner and the skin analysis use Anthropic's Claude, a third-party AI model. What is sent: your message or quiz answers, your skin profile, the product candidates Lumi may suggest from Gloura's catalog, and the photo if there is one. Anthropic may not use this data to train its models. For Lumi chats, Claude also tags each message with a general topic, such as “acne” or “sun protection”. We keep only how many messages fall under each topic, never the message itself, to learn what people ask Lumi. AI answers are general skincare information, not medical advice, and can be wrong; see a dermatologist for skin conditions.

Location

If you allow location access, Gloura reads your approximate location (about 1 km accuracy) while you use the app and sends the coordinates to Open-Meteo, a weather service, to get the current UV index. Your location is not stored on our servers. You can deny location access without losing any other feature.

How We Use It

  • To create your skin analysis, Glow Score and routine
  • To answer your messages to Lumi
  • To show community posts and your saved products
  • To show the UV index
  • To manage your subscription
  • To find and fix bugs and improve the app
  • To measure which of our ads lead to installs and subscriptions

What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell your personal data
  • We don't use your selfie, photos, quiz answers or Lumi chats for advertising
  • We don't use facial recognition or identify you from your face

Who We Share With

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check, and our server and data warehouse): account, app data, selfie storage, usage analytics and crash reports
  • Anthropic (Claude): your selfie and quiz answers for the analysis, your Lumi messages, shared photos and skin profile, and your answers for the routine planner
  • RevenueCat and Apple: subscriptions and payments
  • Meta (Facebook SDK) and TikTok (TikTok Business SDK): the ad events listed above, device and app information, and your advertising identifier if you allow tracking. No selfie, photos or chats
  • Open-Meteo: approximate coordinates, for the UV index

We may also share data when required by law or to protect users from harm.

Your Rights

  • Delete your account: in Profile, tap Delete Account. Your account, selfie, chats, routine and the matching records in our data warehouse are removed
  • Email marketing@koza-lab.co to request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it
  • Turn off camera, photos, location or notifications at any time in your device Settings → Gloura
  • Stop tracking: decline the prompt, or turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Gloura

Data Retention

Account, quiz, selfie, chat, routine and community data: until you delete your account (community posts can also be deleted individually). Usage analytics and crash logs: kept by Google under their standard retention. Location: not stored. Ad measurement events sent to Meta and TikTok are kept under their own privacy policies.

Children

Gloura is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used Gloura, email marketing@koza-lab.co and we will delete the data.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted. Our server only accepts requests from signed-in users of the genuine app, and access rules let you read only your own records.

International Transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country. We rely on their standard contractual protections.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
