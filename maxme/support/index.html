Frequently Asked Questions

How does the face scan work?

MaxMe uses the front camera to take a few frames of your face and analyzes them on your phone. Your photo is never uploaded. The scan looks at grooming, skin, and facial proportions and turns them into scores, a plan, and tips.

How do I get an accurate scan?

Face bright, even light, keep a neutral expression with your mouth closed, take off glasses, and keep hair off your forehead. Hold the phone at arm's length and stay still until the scan finishes. For progress, rescan about once a week in the same light and spot.

Why did my score change between two scans?

Light, angle, and expression affect any photo-based analysis, so scores can move a little between scans. Scan in the same light and position to compare fairly. Scores are a guide for your routine, not a judgement.

How do I delete my scans?

Tap the gear icon on the Face Scan tab to open your Profile, then Delete My Scans. This removes every scan photo and result from your phone.

How does the AI Coach work?

Ask the Coach about grooming, skin, or your routine in the Coach tab. Your message and your latest scan scores (numbers only, never your photo) are sent to our server to write a reply. Answers are general guidance, not medical advice. Free accounts get 2 messages; Premium is unlimited.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the paywall, and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone → Apple ID → Subscriptions → MaxMe → Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium access until the end of your billing period.

Privacy

Your face scan is analyzed on your phone and is never uploaded. We do not sell your data.

  • Scan photos, facial landmarks, and measurements stay on your device and are excluded from backups
  • Onboarding answers are used to build your plan
  • AI Coach requests are deleted automatically after 7 days
  • We use Firebase Analytics and crash reporting to improve the app; analytics contain no face data
  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not see your payment details
