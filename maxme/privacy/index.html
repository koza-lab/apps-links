Privacy Policy

MaxMe · Last updated October 6, 2026

MaxMe is a self-improvement app by Koza Lab that helps you work on grooming, skin and habits. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Your face scan is analyzed on your phone and is never uploaded. Questions? Email marketing@koza-lab.co.

What We Collect

  • Account: your Sign in with Apple identifier and, if you share it, your name and email
  • Onboarding answers: goals, routine level, skin concerns, facial hair, desired look and time commitment, used to build your plan
  • Face scan data: see Face Data (Face Scan) below. It stays on your device
  • Coach messages: what you write to the optional AI Coach
  • Progress and habits: completed tasks and streaks
  • Purchases: subscription status, managed by Apple and RevenueCat
  • Usage and diagnostics: screens and features used, session length, crash logs
  • Approximate location (optional, while using the app): for weather-aware skincare suggestions. You can deny it without losing core features

Face Data (Face Scan)

What we collect

When you run a face scan, MaxMe uses Apple's Vision framework on your device to find your face in the camera image or in a photo you choose. MaxMe keeps one downscaled photo of the scan, the positions of facial landmarks in that photo (such as the outline of your face, eyes, brows, nose and lips) and measurements calculated from them and from your skin colour (proportions, skin tone, redness and texture). MaxMe does not create a 3D face model or a face print, and does not use your face to identify or authenticate you.

How we use it

This face data is used only to calculate your scan results, draw guide lines on your own photo and show your progress compared with your earlier scans. It is not used for advertising or tracking, to identify you, or to train any model.

Where it is stored and who can access it

Your scan photo, landmarks and measurements are processed and stored only on your device, in MaxMe's private storage. They are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. They are never uploaded to MaxMe's servers and are not shared with or sold to any third party. Our usage analytics contain no face data. For a completed scan we record only technical information and your overall score rounded to a 10-point range.

How long we keep it

Your face data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete all scans at any time in Settings → Delete My Scans. Logging out or deleting your account also removes all scans, and so does deleting the app. MaxMe keeps at most your 20 most recent scans and deletes older ones automatically.

AI Coach

If you send a message to the optional AI Coach, your message and your latest scan scores (numbers only) are sent to MaxMe's server and processed by OpenAI to write a reply. Your photo, landmarks and measurements are never sent. Coach requests are deleted automatically after 7 days, or immediately when you delete your account.

How We Use It

  • To build and adjust your personal plan and daily tasks
  • To show your scan results and progress on your device
  • To answer your AI Coach messages
  • To manage your subscription
  • To find and fix bugs and improve the app

What We Don't Do

  • We don't upload, sell or share your face photos or face measurements
  • We don't sell your personal data
  • We don't use your data for third-party advertising
  • We don't use facial recognition or identify you from your face

Who We Share With

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check): account, app data, usage analytics and crash reports. No face data
  • OpenAI: AI Coach messages and your latest scan scores (numbers only), to write coach replies
  • RevenueCat and Apple: subscriptions and payments

Your Rights

  • Delete your scans: Settings → Delete My Scans
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete Account. Your account data is removed from our servers, and your scans are removed from your device
  • Email marketing@koza-lab.co to request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it
  • Turn off camera, location or notifications at any time in your device Settings → MaxMe

Data Retention

Face scan data: on your device until you delete it (see above). Coach requests: 7 days. Account, onboarding and progress data: until you delete your account. Usage analytics: up to 14 months. Crash logs: up to 90 days. Location: not stored.

Children

MaxMe is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used MaxMe, email marketing@koza-lab.co and we will delete the data.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted, and data on our servers is protected by access rules so only you can read your own records. Scan data on your device uses iOS file protection.

International Transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country. We rely on their standard contractual protections.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
